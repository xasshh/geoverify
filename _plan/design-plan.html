What each colour is allowed to mean

Gold is the only accent, and it means verification and attention: the active cell, the confirmed identity, the capture point. It is never used to make a panel look less flat. The other four are states, and carry a shape and a word as well as a hue, so the console is readable at a glance and to a colourblind supervisor:

Accepted Needs review Rejected In progress Unassigned

Circle, triangle, diamond, square, ring, each fixed to meaning, never reused. A supervisor learns five shapes once.

Why this pairing and not the obvious one

The obvious move for a credibility product is a neutral grotesque everywhere: Inter, or a government-issue face like Public Sans. It is safe, and it produces precisely the anonymous surface that got the last build rejected. The other obvious move, right now, is Instrument Serif over a warm cream ground; it is so current that an evaluator has seen it on forty landing pages this year, which reads as generated rather than designed.

Newsreader is a Scotch Roman: the lineage of printed gazettes, land registers and survey reports, which is exactly what this product is. It has sharp bracketed serifs and a genuinely idiosyncratic italic, it holds at 42px on an evidence-pack cover and at 15px in a subheading, and it is open-licensed and self-hostable, so no field device ever waits on a font CDN. IBM Plex Sans was drawn for a company that makes instruments; it has true tabular figures, a real 450/500 weight range for dense tables, and it pairs with IBM Plex Mono as one designed system, so coordinates and H3 indices look like the machine values they are without a second family fighting the first.

The signature: the Presence Mark

§8 · one idea

Every record in this system carries a small hexagon containing the officer's actual walking trace at the moment of capture, drawn from the LineStringM, with a gold dot at the capture point.

It is generated, never designed: same data, same mark, every time. No two are alike because no two walks are alike. It is the product's claim rendered as a shape: someone was here, and this is the path they took to get here.

It is not decoration. It is the anti-spoofing affordance

§6 asks for "trace naturalness" as a flag: perfectly straight traces, zero jitter, identical repeating accuracy values. That check runs server-side and produces a number. The Presence Mark makes the same fact pre-attentive: a supervisor scanning a column of 200 marks finds the fabricated day without opening a single record.

● Walked
Street-grid turns, dwell clusters at each capture, jitter between fixes.
confidence 88
◆ Fabricated
Straight segments, uniform spacing, no dwell, no jitter.
confidence 21 · flagged

Where it appears

  • 16px: a review-queue column. At this size it carries density, not route: a walked day reads as a dense figure, a fabricated one as a line. That is the only claim I'd make for it, and it is the one the column needs.
  • 40px: the record card and the officer's own capture list.
  • 240px: the review screen's left column, where it is the primary evidence.
  • Full plate: the cover of the PDF evidence pack handed to a client's auditor.

Motion, once, where it explains: on the review screen the trace draws itself along its own timestamps, so the supervisor watches the officer's day at 40× and sees the pauses. Nowhere else does it animate, and it respects prefers-reduced-motion.

Self-critique

§8 · what I'd have produced for anyone
01Your palette sits inside the generated-design cluster, and I nearly didn't say so
Warm off-white ground, serif display, gold-bronze accent. That is close to the single most common look produced by design tools right now. The brief's own tokens land there. It would have been easy to accept them silently because they came from you. Kept, with the reason made explicit. What separates this from the cluster is not the hue, it is that colour is never decorative here: gold means verification, and a panel that looks flat stays flat. Plus the density, the tabular discipline, and the dark field surface as the primary environment. If those three slip during the build, the palette will read as generated and the fix will not be a new colour.
02"The map is the interface, chrome floats over it"
This is every mapping product ever made, and I wrote it down without thinking because the brief said it. Changed to a cell-locked work list. This map is not for exploring. It is bounded by the assignment, every footprint carries a visited state, the denominator is permanently on screen, and leaving the cell is an explicit logged action. That is an enumeration instrument, not a map viewer.
03A dark field app because dark looks technical
I defaulted to the dark surface and was ready to justify it with "sunlight legibility". That is backwards: in direct sunlight a bright screen with a light ground is generally more readable, because phone displays cannot out-emit the sun. Dark saves OLED battery. Those are two different arguments and I was about to merge them into one convenient one. Two real modes, switched by physics not taste. A Daylight mode: light ground, maximum brightness, heaviest weights, the AA-checked light palette, auto-engaged via the ambient light sensor with a manual override that sticks. A Dusk mode: the dark palette and a dimmed map style, for battery and low light. Both are designed, neither is a filter over the other, and the officer can pin either.
04A three-pane review screen
Evidence left, form right, actions bottom, the layout of every admin moderation tool. Re-ordered around the supervisor's actual questions: was the officer there, is this the right building, is the record sane. Flags also moved out of a sidebar to sit against the evidence that produced them. Same three panes; the information design underneath is different.
05The bottom sheet as an edit form
Standard mobile pattern, and wrong for this product: an edit form implies overwriting, and §3 forbids it. Made an observation form. Prior state is visible, greyed and non-editable, below the current entry. The officer records what is true today; March stays where it is.

One schema change I want to argue for

§3 · re-enumeration

§3 requires that "re-enumeration creates a new observation, never an overwrite", but §3's own table list puts the time-varying attributes (floors, condition, occupancy_status, operating_status, employee_band) directly on structures and enterprises. Those two things cannot both be true. A second visit either overwrites the column or it does not happen.

Proposal, which keeps your schema literally intact: add structure_observations and enterprise_observations as the append-only authority: one row per visit, carrying the mutable attributes plus its own provenance (captured_by, captured_at, field_session_id, assignment_id, capture_accuracy_m, confidence_score). The columns on structures stay exactly as specified and become a projection of the latest accepted observation, maintained on write.

You get simple queries for every ordinary read, "what did this look like in March" as a first-class question, and no hard deletes anywhere. The cost is one extra write per capture and a projection to keep honest, which is a test, not a risk.

Two smaller additions in the same spirit:

  • consent_records: §10 requires NDPA consent captured at the point of collection. That needs to be its own evidenced row (script version, granted, recorded_by, recorded_at, lawful basis, purpose), not a boolean on an enterprise.
  • isic_classes + trade_aliases: the ISIC tree, plus the Nigerian trade names the officer actually searches ("POS operator", "vulcanizer", "provisions store") mapped onto it. The alias list is the thing that makes field capture fast and is the thing we will keep tuning.

Battery, since you asked

  • Adaptive trace interval. 5 s / 10 m while moving; backs off to 30 s once fixes cluster inside the accuracy radius; resumes instantly on movement. A stationary officer is the common case during a long enterprise interview.
  • Fixes never touch React state. They go to a ref and drive the marker imperatively, so a GPS update does not re-render a tree.
  • Batched IndexedDB writes: one transaction per N fixes rather than one per fix.
  • Dusk map style: fewer layers, no 3D, no hillshade, maxPitch: 0, no continuous render loop.
  • Photo encode off the main thread, once, at capture.
  • Uploads default to Wi-Fi or charging, with a manual "send now" override that is always available.
  • Wake lock only while a session is active, released the moment it ends. If the lock is denied, the officer is told plainly, because a locked screen means a lost trace.

What I'd flag before we start

honest list
h3-pg on PostgreSQL 17 is the one unproven dependency

Your Postgres is Homebrew 17.10 and h3-pg has to be compiled against it. I expect it to work, but it is a source build of a third-party extension and it is the foundation of M2. I will prove it in the first hour of M0 and tell you immediately if it fails, rather than discovering it three milestones deep. Fallback if it will not build: PostGIS ST_HexagonGrid for cell geometry with H3 indices assigned by a small C-backed helper. Worse, and I would come back to you before taking it.

Docker ships but cannot be tested here

No Docker runtime on this machine, so the compose stack is written and reviewed but not executed locally. Development runs native against your PG17 + Redis; MinIO is replaced in dev by a private local disk behind the same signed-URL contract, so no application code differs between dev and production. The compose file gets its first real run in CI.

PHP 8.5, not 8.3

Your runtime is 8.5.0. I will constrain to ^8.3 so the brief's target is honoured and CI can pin 8.3, but local development runs 8.5. If any dependency objects, you will hear about it in M0.

Play Integrity needs a real Android package

§6 wants a device-integrity verdict at session start. That requires a signed Android app registered with Google Play, which a PWA is not. Until there is a TWA wrapper, that signal will be recorded as unverified and weighted accordingly, and it will not silently score as passing. Worth deciding whether a TWA is in scope, because it is the difference between a real integrity check and an absent one.

Google Open Buildings coverage for AMAC needs confirming

Coverage and vintage vary by tile. First task in M2 is to pull the AMAC tiles and report the actual footprint count and confidence distribution, because the whole denominator argument rests on it. If density looks wrong, Microsoft's layer is the cross-check.
